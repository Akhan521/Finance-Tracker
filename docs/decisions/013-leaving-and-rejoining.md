# D13. Leaving and rejoining a household: Decided

**Decision.**
- **Leaving is a deliberate choice by the member.** They intentionally choose
  to leave; it never happens by accident.
- **After leaving, they keep their account and their personal bills.** They
  lose access to the household bills, so they see only their own account and
  personal bills.
- **They can rejoin later.** For example, someone who moved out may still want
  to follow the household bills.
- **History stays accurate (Claude's call, matches the guess Aamir agreed
  with).** Past household payments still show who paid them, even after that
  person leaves.

- **Rejoining needs approval from an owner (see D14).** A former member can't
  let themselves back in. This closes the gap where anyone ever in the
  household could see its bills again whenever they liked.
- **Joining for the first time works the same way.** An owner invites or
  approves. Joining and rejoining use one flow.

**Update (2026-10-25, see D19).** Rejoining happens by an owner inviting the
person again. There's no separate "request to rejoin" step.
