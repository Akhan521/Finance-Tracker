# D15. Deleting an account: Decided (Claude's recommendation, Aamir asked for it)

**Difference from leaving (D13).** Leaving a household keeps the account and
personal bills. Deleting the account removes the person entirely.

**Decision.**
- **Personal bills are deleted right away**, not kept for the 3-year window.
- **Their name is removed from household history.** Past household payments
  they made show "former member." Amounts, dates, and bills are kept, so the
  household's record stays complete.
- **A clear warning first.** The app explains that deletion is permanent and
  offers to export their personal bills before confirming.
- **If they're the last owner,** they must hand ownership to someone before
  deleting (same rule as leaving, D14).

**Why.** Under security-first (D6), "delete my account" should mean the person
is gone, not that their name quietly stays behind. The household loses very
little: the payment facts remain, and members remember who it was. Leaving
(D13) is the option for someone who only wants out of the household.

**Honest caveat.** The hosting provider keeps its own short-term backups, so
deleted data can linger there for a limited time before it ages out. The app
shouldn't claim deletion is instant everywhere. Check the chosen provider's
backup retention when picking the stack.
